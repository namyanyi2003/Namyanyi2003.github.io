## Volumes XI–XX

These ten volumes extend the Symbolic Mechanics runtime into **intimacy-boundary computation, projection runtime, post-projection alarm locking, shame-origin rupture, clown-system architecture, and dark-field survival navigation**. Read in sequence, they define how the room opens, projects, overheats, destabilizes, locks, reorganizes, and continues after catastrophic shame and projection failure.

- [Volume XI — Intimacy as a Boundary Event, V × G Opening, and Δ Directional Alignment](./volume-11.md)  
  *Boundary-opening and gate-configuration layer*  
  Most theories describe intimacy as openness, attachment style, or emotional closeness, but do not specify the mechanical boundary event through which intimacy actually begins. Volume XI formalizes that missing opening layer by modelling intimacy as a **V × G** boundary event, assigning **Δ** directional authority only after opening becomes possible, and showing how closure generates **Return Load** when visibility rises again.

- [Volume XII — Force Distribution, Boundary Parameters, and the Mechanics of Intimacy Entry](./volume-12.md)  
  *Boundary-parameterization and entry-selection layer*  
  Most theories describe intimacy boundaries through attachment or temperament, but do not specify how the room acquires the parameters that govern entry. Volume XII formalizes that missing parameter layer by modelling parents as generators of **V**, **G**, and **A_th**, showing how force distribution is first mapped and later fixed through adolescent recalibration, and defining adult intimacy as execution on steady-state boundary parameters.

- [Volume XIII — Projection, the Projector, and the First Optical Event of Intimacy](./volume-13.md)  
  *Projection-initiation and optical-entry layer*  
  Most theories describe projection as fantasy or distortion, but do not specify the mechanical function projection serves at the beginning of intimacy. Volume XIII formalizes that missing optical-entry layer by modelling projection as the room’s first internally generated image-field, using the projector and a placeholder interface to allow contact before full reality-processing becomes available.

- [Volume XIV — Projection as Single-Input Boundary Physics, the Table-Surface, and Reality Occlusion](./volume-14.md)  
  *Single-input projection and occlusion layer*  
  Most theories describe projection as idealization or misreading, but do not specify the boundary condition under which projection becomes mechanically self-sustaining. Volume XIV formalizes that missing projection-physics layer by modelling **single-input mode**, **table-surface integration**, **ice-water correction**, and **occlusion** as the mechanics that temporarily let one internal image outrank the rest of reality.

- [Volume XV — Projection Breakdown, Thermal Overload, and the Closed Mechanical Loop](./volume-15.md)  
  *Projection-breakdown and thermal-overload layer*  
  Most theories describe relational rupture or disillusionment as emotional disappointment, but do not specify the closed loop by which a projected world breaks down. Volume XV formalizes that missing breakdown layer by modelling **projector heat**, **Position-3 melt**, **vapor pressure**, **Bartender delay**, and **automatic shutdown** as the deterministic thermal endpoint of single-input intimacy.

- [Volume XVI — Alarm Takeover, Mirrored Alarm Recruitment, and Symbolic Locking](./volume-16.md)  
  *Post-projection alarm-governance layer*  
  Most theories describe the aftermath of collapse as shock, panic, or conflict, but do not specify the first governing state that appears once projection has shut down. Volume XVI formalizes that missing post-projection layer by modelling **Alarm Takeover**, **Double-Alarm Coupling**, and **Symbolic Locking** as the mechanics that govern the room when projection ends but the symbolic field remains seated and active.

- [Volume XVII — Catastrophic Shame, the Shadow-Child, and the First Rupture Template of Intimate Selection](./volume-17.md)  
  *Shame-origin and rupture-template layer*  
  Most theories describe shame as emotion, self-evaluation, or wounded identity, but do not specify the deeper structural break from which shame-driven mechanics arise. Volume XVII formalizes that missing origin layer by modelling catastrophic shame as the first misalignment among **Self-Body**, **SCr**, and **Inner System**, and by defining the **Shadow-Child** as the residual rupture artifact that later organizes intimate recognition.

- [Volume XVIII — The Clown Subsystem, Dark-Field Action-Formation, and the Search for the Original Shame Source](./volume-18.md)  
  *Shadow–clown subsystem and lighting-governance layer*  
  Most theories describe masking, defensive performance, or fixation in emotional terms, but do not specify the agency-capable subsystem that forms after shame disappears into the dark field. Volume XVIII formalizes that missing subsystem layer by modelling the **Clown** as shame converted into agency, with root access to lighting architecture, attraction locking, and rupture-pattern search.

- [Volume XIX — The Grand Hall, Scene-Shift Mechanics, and the Clown as the Deep Engine](./volume-19.md)  
  *Grand-hall and deep-engine layer*  
  Most theories describe role-shifting, avoidance, or cyclical return in narrative or personality terms, but do not specify the architecture in which large-scale scene movement occurs. Volume XIX formalizes that missing hall-scale engine layer by modelling the **Grand Hall** as the system’s real operating environment and the **Clown** as the deep engine that governs scene-shifting, dark-run continuity, and repeated return to the intimacy stage.

- [Volume XX — The Firefly, Post-Shame Survival Navigation, and the Formation of the Compensation Vector](./volume-20.md)  
  *Primitive navigational and post-shame survival layer*  
  Most theories describe instinct, intuition, or hope in reflective terms, but do not specify the minimal directional signal that survives after higher-order structure collapses. Volume XX formalizes that missing primitive layer by modelling the **Firefly** as the system’s post-shame compass and by defining the **Firefly–Clown–Self-Core triad** as the architecture that enables survival navigation, switching, and long-term compensation-vector formation.
