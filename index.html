A symbolic-computational engine for mapping human situations and predicting next-state dynamics.

Human OS turns ordinary human situations into structured state maps. It tracks pressure loops, hidden transition points, and likely next-state movement before visible behavior fully stabilizes.

What Human OS Does

Situation Mapping

Human OS turns ordinary human language into a structured map of the situation: actors, repeated events, pressure points, interaction loops, missing evidence, and likely dynamic direction.

Internal-State Tracking

Human OS tracks how a small external signal becomes a heavy internal event. It asks what entered, what changed internally, and whether the same signal is becoming heavier over time.

Loop Detection

Human OS identifies repeated loops before they become fixed patterns. It tracks how pressure, reaction, delay, and repeated signals form a loop.

Next-State Prediction

Human OS predicts likely next-state dynamics if the current structure continues. Prediction means likely state movement, not fixed destiny.

Plain-Language Output

Internal computation can be complex. External output must be human-readable. Normal user-facing output should explain the map, loop, and likely next state in ordinary language.

Example: From Situation to Next State

Input“A homework situation repeats every day and anger appears every day.”
Situation MapThis looks less like a one-time homework refusal and more like a repeated task-pressure loop.
Active Loop
Homework time appears → demand or expectation rises → resistance appears → pressure increases → anger becomes the exit → the task is delayed → the next homework signal becomes heavier
Next-State PredictionIf the loop continues unchanged, the next likely state is earlier resistance.
BoundaryThis is a cautious structural reading, not a fixed judgment.
