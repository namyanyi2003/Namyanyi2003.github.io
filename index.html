Human OS

Map the human situation.
Predict the next state.

A symbolic-computational engine for mapping human situations and predicting next-state dynamics.

Human OS turns ordinary human situations into structured state maps. It tracks pressure loops, hidden transition points, and likely next-state movement before visible behavior fully stabilizes.

What Human OS Does

Human OS is presented as a model first. The theory archive, benchmark reports, and runtime traces support this model rather than replacing it.

Situation Mapping

Turns ordinary human language into a structured map of actors, repeated events, pressure points, interaction loops, missing evidence, and likely dynamic direction.

Internal-State Tracking

Tracks how a small external signal becomes a heavy internal event, what entered, what changed, and whether the same signal is getting heavier over time.

Loop Detection

Identifies repeated loops before they become fixed patterns by tracking pressure, reaction, delay, and repeated signals.

Next-State Prediction

Predicts likely next-state dynamics if the current structure continues. Prediction means likely state movement, not fixed destiny.

Plain-Language Output

Turns internal structure into readable output that explains the map, loop, and likely next state in ordinary language.

Example: From Situation to Next State

Input“A homework situation repeats every day and anger appears every day.”
Situation MapThis looks less like a one-time homework refusal and more like a repeated task-pressure loop.
Active Loop
Homework time appears → demand or expectation rises → resistance appears → pressure increases → anger becomes the exit → the task is delayed → the next homework signal becomes heavier
Next-State PredictionIf the loop continues unchanged, the next likely state is earlier resistance.
BoundaryThis is a cautious structural reading, not a fixed judgment.
