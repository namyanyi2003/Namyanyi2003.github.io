## Research Hook

Most theoretical systems describe themes, concepts, or local mechanisms, but do not provide a unified engine-level specification that makes the whole architecture computable as one deterministic system.

This document formalizes that missing layer by consolidating **Volumes I–XX** into a single canonical specification of the Symbolic Mechanics engine.

- system stratification

## Overview

This document is the formal engine-level consolidation of Volumes I–XX.

It defines the first twenty volumes not as isolated topics, but as one multi-layer runtime architecture. In this specification, the system is stratified into eight layers:

- **Layer 0 — Core Engine**
- **Layer 1 — Symbol Economy**
- **Layer 2 — Axis Geometry**
- **Layer 3 — Boundary Physics**
- **Layer 4 — Interaction Field**
- **Layer 5 — Projection Runtime**
