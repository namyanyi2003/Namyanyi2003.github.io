Reading note

How this site should be used

This site should not be approached as a general theory homepage, a motivational overview, or a simplified introduction. Its structure is closer to a public research surface: specification, clustered publication pages, and archive continuity.

The most accurate reading order for formal readers is usually specification first, then the clustered volume pages, then individual releases where needed. Readers who prefer developmental order may reverse that path and move from Volumes I–X into Volumes XI–XX before returning to the integrated specification.

The public archive layer exists to preserve coherence across release surfaces. The website is therefore best read as a curated interface to a structured system, not as a collection of isolated essays.

Why this page matters

Research pages often fail by mixing explanation, promotion, and archive logic into one blurred surface. This page separates those functions and clarifies where the formal entry points actually are.
