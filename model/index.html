Core Model Concepts

A compact glossary for the public model layer.

Situation Map

The visible situation and the structure forming inside it.

Surface / Core Separation

The difference between surface wording and inner movement.

Pressure Movement

Where pressure enters, grows, redirects, or becomes heavier.

Active Loop

The repeated movement currently running or becoming more likely.

Next-State Direction

The likely next movement if the structure continues.

Uncertainty Boundary

The missing evidence and conditions that could change the reading.
