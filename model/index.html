Human OS Model

Map the human situation.
Predict the next state.

Human OS is a symbolic-computational model for mapping human situations into structured state maps and predicting likely next-state dynamics before visible behavior fully stabilizes.

Core Model Question

What changes inside a human situation before the next visible action appears?

A chatbot usually answers what was said. Human OS maps what is changing underneath the situation.

Core Concepts

These are the model-level concepts that connect the theory archive, runtime line, simulator direction, and benchmark evidence into one readable architecture.

1. Situation Map

What is the visible situation, who is inside it, and what structure is forming?

2. Surface / Core Separation

What is said on the surface, and what state movement may be forming underneath?

3. Pressure Movement

Where does pressure enter, accumulate, redirect, or become heavier?

4. Active Loop

What repeated loop is currently running or becoming more likely to repeat?

5. Next-State Direction

If the structure continues, what internal or relational state is likely to appear next?

6. Uncertainty Boundary

What evidence is missing, and what could change the prediction?

Model Output Anatomy

Human OS output is not just a response. It is a structured reading of situation, loop, direction, and uncertainty.

Input SituationA person receives a neutral message from someone they care about: “Okay, we can talk later.”
Situation MapA neutral message enters an already pressure-loaded relational field.
Surface / Core SeparationThe surface signal is delay. The core movement may be uncertainty, priority checking, or withdrawal detection.
Active Loop
waiting → checking → rereading → pressure rises → later signals become heavier
Next-State PredictionIf the loop continues, the next neutral signal may be read as stronger evidence of distance.
Uncertainty BoundaryPrediction depends on prior relationship pattern, later confirming signals, and whether the delay is repaired.

What Human OS Does Not Claim

The model is intentionally bounded. Prediction means likely state dynamics under bounded input conditions, not certainty about future behavior.
