## Volumes I–X

These ten volumes establish the early architecture of the system, from kernel logic to companion stabilization, boundary geometry, visibility collapse, attraction fields, intrusion physics, blackout topology, and voluntary shutdown.

- [Volume I — Canonical Engine Kernel](./volume-01.md)  
  *Kernel layer*  
  Most theories describe inner life, but do not specify the irreducible engine through which internal events must pass. Volume I formalizes that missing kernel by defining the deterministic loop **Δ → S → L → R → Exit**.

- [Volume II — Structural Encoding](./volume-02.md)  
  *Encoding layer*  
  Most theories describe feeling, memory, or interpretation, but do not specify how symbolic material becomes fixed into internal structure. Volume II formalizes that missing encoding layer by modelling structural inscription as the bridge between event input and durable system form.

- [Volume III — Shadow-Load Formation and Collapse Dynamics](./volume-03.md)  
  *Load / collapse layer*  
  Most theories describe distress, repression, or breakdown, but do not specify how unresolved symbolic material becomes accumulating internal burden. Volume III formalizes that missing pressure layer by modelling shadow-load as the bridge between encoded structure and collapse-capable mechanics.

- [Volume IV — Companion Module, Three-Exit Architecture, and Regenerative Exit Mechanics](./volume-04.md)  
  *Companion-module and regenerative-exit layer*  
  Most theories describe support or healing in emotional terms, but do not specify the mechanical condition under which a symbolic system gains a lower-energy route of continuation. Volume IV formalizes that missing stabilizer layer by defining parental volume across the M-axis and F-axis, modelling the emergence of the C-Module, and showing how a three-exit architecture with regenerative routing becomes mechanically possible.

- [Volume V — Boundary Dynamics, Safe-Proximity Radius, and Displacement Mechanics](./volume-05.md)  
  *Boundary geometry and change-permission layer*  
  Most theories explain failed change in emotional or motivational terms, but do not specify the boundary geometry that makes forward movement mechanically impossible even when intention is present. Volume V formalizes that missing displacement layer by modelling **Rₛ** as a structural variable, defining change failure as vector conflict, and showing that actual change begins only through **C↓ → ΔO_micro → Rₛ↑**.

- [Volume VI — Visibility Collapse, Sorting Failure, and the Mechanics of Existence Compensation](./volume-06.md)  
  *Visibility-collapse and existence-compensation layer*  
  Most theories describe confusion, emptiness, or validation-seeking as emotional or cognitive phenomena, but do not specify the mechanical condition under which symbolic weight becomes unreadable. Volume VI formalizes that missing visibility-failure layer by defining sorting failure as an S-side readability collapse and modelling existence-compensation as the fallback procedure that appears when weight signals go offline.

- [Volume VII — Attraction Tension Mechanics](./volume-07.md)  
  *Attraction-tension and oscillatory field layer*  
  Most theories describe attraction as emotion, chemistry, or preference, but do not specify the field conditions under which attraction becomes persistent tension. Volume VII formalizes that missing field layer by modelling attraction tension as the deterministic output of **ΔV**, **Rₛ**, **Eₕ**, and **Eₓ** interacting inside a single oscillatory system.

- [Volume VIII — Framework Initialization, Structural Intrusion, and Alarm-Cascade Mechanics](./volume-08.md)  
  *Intrusion-physics and alarm-cascade layer*  
  Most theories describe resistance to advice or commands in emotional or interpersonal terms, but do not specify the condition under which an external directive becomes a structural threat to sovereignty. Volume VIII formalizes that missing intrusion layer by defining command as **I°**, modelling Alarm activation under Seat-weight and radius conditions, and showing how repeated intrusion reshapes visibility, sovereignty, and exit bias over time.

- [Volume IX — Black Room Mechanics, Orb Dynamics, and Compulsive Continuation](./volume-09.md)  
  *Blackout-state and attractor-governance layer*  
  Most theories describe compulsion, addiction, or fixation through desire, reward, or habit, but do not specify the blackout-state in which continuation becomes mechanically inevitable. Volume IX formalizes that missing blackout layer by modelling the Black Room as a forced state transition and the Orb as a rotational attractor that captures attention, suspends symbolic access, and governs behaviour until light returns.

- [Volume X — Voluntary Boundary Shutdown and the Mechanics of Existential Supplementation](./volume-10.md)  
  *Voluntary-shutdown and existential-supplementation layer*  
  Most theories describe withdrawal, fantasy, sensual escape, or sexual opening under pressure as collapse or addiction, but do not specify the condition under which the system deliberately turns off its own boundary-light. Volume X formalizes that missing shutdown layer by modelling voluntary boundary shutdown as a protective override that suspends visibility, generates an attractor corridor, and postpones shame until symbolic reconvergence returns.
