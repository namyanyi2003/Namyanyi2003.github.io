Benchmark Line Positioning

The benchmark line is an evaluation surface. It compares interpretive performance under fixed constraints and preserved corpora. It does not function as clinical validation, therapeutic outcome study, or commercial benchmark claim.

Benchmark records sit beside the specification, theory-publication, and prototype lines as a distinct public evidence route.
